      HOW IT WORKS

      Local Beam Search keeps several candidate tours. Each round swaps pairs of stops and retains the shortest candidates. Then 2-opt reverses segments when that shortens the trip.

      The seed makes the search repeatable. Larger beams explore more candidates, but can take longer. A heuristic route is not a proof of optimality.
